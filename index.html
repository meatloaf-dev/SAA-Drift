About this atlas

Click anywhere on the map to probe the field at that point. Use the slider or ▶ to scrub through 130 years of geomagnetic change — watch the anomaly deepen and march west, and the north magnetic pole race from Arctic Canada toward Siberia. The final four years (2027–2030) are a published forecast, not a measurement — shown with a dashed badge and boundary.

Core field model, 1997–2026 ESA Swarm MCO_SHA_2X product (CHAOS‑Core, DTU Space) — fit to real Swarm, CryoSat‑2, Ørsted and ground‑observatory magnetic measurements. Queried from ESA's vires.services API on a recurring schedule, so this stays current without a manual rebuild.
Core field model, 1900–1995 IGRF‑14 (IAGA / NOAA NCEI), the public reference model, evaluated at the same 460 km altitude for historical continuity before Swarm's launch — also via vires.services.
Forecast, 2027–2030 IGRF‑14's predictive secular‑variation segment — the same kind of 5‑year-ahead forecast NOAA/BGS publish as the World Magnetic Model. A projection of rate, shape and trajectory, not an observation.
Magnetic north pole track NOAA NCEI historical & predicted dip‑pole positions, 1590–2025 (ngdc.noaa.gov/geomag/data/poles).
Coastlines & borders Natural Earth 1:10m admin‑0 countries, via world-atlas — coastline and political borders separated using shared‑edge topology, simplified from ~480k to ~70k points.
City & postal code gazetteer GeoNames cities15000 (all ~34,000 places over 15,000 population) and US.zip postal codes, CC BY 4.0.
Real satellite pass (toggle, field layer) Actual 1‑Hz Swarm‑A MAGx_LR Level‑1b measurements for 2026‑11‑03 — not a model, the real instrument reading, same file anyone can pull from swarm-diss.eo.esa.int.
Close-zoom isogonic lines CHAOS core field plus its static crustal (lithospheric) field to degree/order 185 — internal sources only, so the lines aren't stamped with one instant's magnetospheric ring-current state. This includes real crustal-field structure the coarser models above can't see, not just finer interpolation of the same smooth field. Evaluated fresh each refresh on a 0.5° grid at the ellipsoid surface, then contoured at 0.25° resolution once you zoom in past ~6° of visible arc.

The grids above are generated offline on a recurring schedule by querying ESA's VirES API (vires.services) with a personal access token, then committed as the static data files this page loads — the page itself makes no live network calls and needs no login from you. Raw per‑satellite telemetry (Level‑1b/2 CDF products) is also available from VirES and ESA's open dissemination server at swarm-diss.eo.esa.int for anyone who wants to go a layer deeper.
